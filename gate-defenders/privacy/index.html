Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · Last updated: 28 October 2026
Applies to the mobile game “Gate Defenders” (package name: com.yemregames.gatedefenders)

Summary.

  • The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or telephone number. Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Personalized ads are shown only with your explicit consent and never to users under 18. Otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • With your explicit consent only (and never for users under 18), the Game sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. This is switched off until you agree.
  • You can change your choices at any time under Settings → Ad Preferences.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how personal data is processed in connection with the mobile game Gate Defenders (the “Game”), published by Yemre Games (“we”, “us” or “our”). It applies to the version of the Game distributed through Google Play.

2. Data Controller and Contact Details

Yemre Games is the developer name used by Yunus Emre Güngör, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the processing described in this Policy. You can contact us at yemregames.dev@gmail.com.

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party:

  • your gameplay progress (levels, stars, gold, upgrades, daily rewards and quests, Endless Defense records and statistics) and settings (audio, vibration, language);
  • the year of birth you enter when you first start the Game. It is used only on your device to choose ads that are appropriate for your age and to decide which questions you are asked; it is not sent anywhere;
  • a record of your privacy choices (what you chose, the version of the text you answered and the date), so that we can respect and demonstrate your choices.

This information is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is financed by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects from your device: your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), information about your interactions with ads (for example ad views, clicks and rewards) and diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK. Google processes this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Personalized ads (ads based on your interests) are shown only if you are 18 or older and have given your explicit consent. You are asked after your year of birth and the privacy notice.
  • If you do not consent, or if you are under 18, ads are still shown in the Game, but they are non-personalized: they are selected on the basis of the content of the Game and your approximate location, and your advertising ID is used only for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • For users who indicate an age under 18 we request age-appropriate ad treatment from Google (under 13: treatment as a child; 13–17: treatment as a teen), and all ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (maximum content rating “PG”).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: your choices are collected through Google’s consent form (a Google-certified consent management platform using the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework), which appears instead of our own question. If you do not give consent there, Google may show only limited ads, which do not use your advertising ID for ad selection.
  • Ads are never shown while you are playing a level. An interstitial ad may be shown when a level ends; rewarded ads (doubling a level reward, or continuing after being defeated) are shown only if you choose to watch them.

5. Game Improvement Data (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics)

If you are 18 or older and give your explicit consent to “Game improvement data”, the Game sends the following information to Google through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. This collection is switched off by default and starts only after you agree; it is never switched on for users under 18.

  • Gameplay events: levels started and finished (result, stars, gold earned and, in Endless Defense, the wave reached), continues used after being defeated, gold earned from rewarded ads and rewarded ads watched; and general settings of the Game (language and the highest level reached).
  • Identifiers and technical data: app instance ID and Firebase installation ID, device model and operating system version, app version, language, session information, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. The advertising ID is used by Analytics only if you have also consented to personalized ads.
  • Crash reports: when the Game crashes or an error occurs, the error’s stack trace, relevant app state, device metadata (for example model, operating system version, available memory) and a Crashlytics installation ID.

We use this information only to understand how the Game is played (for example, at which level players get stuck), to improve game balance and to find and fix errors. We do not send your name, e-mail address, year of birth or precise location. The data is used in aggregated form; we do not try to identify individual players. Google processes the data as our service provider under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase. In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, the consent choices you make in Google’s consent form also limit how Analytics may store and use identifiers (Google’s consent mode).

6. Data Transmitted Before Your Decision

Until you answer the “Game improvement data” question, no gameplay statistics or crash reports are sent. Gameplay events that occur while the privacy questions are open (for example, the start of the first level) are kept only temporarily in the device’s memory; they are sent only if you consent and are discarded if you do not.

7. Google Play

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information relating to your download and use of the Game in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. If you have enabled the sharing of usage and diagnostics data on your device, Google may provide us with aggregated statistics on the stability and performance of the Game (for example crash and “application not responding” rates) that do not identify individual users.

10. Retention

  • Information stored on your device (section 3): until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event data is kept for up to 14 months after your last activity in the Game, after which it is deleted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and associated identifiers are kept for 90 days.
  • Firebase installation IDs: until they are deleted at our request; Google then removes them from its systems within 180 days.
  • Google AdMob: Google retains advertising data in accordance with its own retention policy.

11. Your Choices

  • Change or withdraw your consent: Settings → Ad Preferences in the Game (personalized ads and game improvement data). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland this opens Google’s consent form, followed by the game improvement data question. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Local data: uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

12. Your Rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it is; to learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in accordance with that purpose; to know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad; to request rectification if it is incomplete or inaccurate; to request its erasure or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 and the notification of these operations to the third parties to whom it was transferred; to object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems; and to claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, write to yemregames.dev@gmail.com. Because the Game does not ask for your name or e-mail address, please describe your request and, if possible, your device’s advertising ID or the approximate date you installed the Game, so that we can identify the relevant data; we will respond within 30 days at the latest. Where data is held by Google as an independent controller (for example for advertising), you can also use the tools in your Google account and device settings. You may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) in Türkiye or with the data protection authority in your country.

13. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The Game asks for your year of birth when you first start it. Users under 18 are never asked for consent to personalized ads or game improvement data, never receive personalized ads and are never included in game improvement data. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data in connection with the Game, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

14. Security

All data sent by the Game and by the services listed above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS). We do not operate our own servers that store your data.

15. Changes to This Policy

We update this Policy when the Game or the services it uses change, before the corresponding version of the Game is released. If a change affects what you have consented to, the Game will ask you again. The “Last updated” date above shows when this Policy was last revised.

16. Contact

For any questions or requests concerning this Policy or your privacy, please contact us at yemregames.dev@gmail.com.

Oyun ilk açılışta doğum yılınızı sorar. 18 yaşından küçük kullanıcılara kişiselleştirilmiş reklam veya oyunu iyileştirme verileri için hiçbir zaman açık rıza sorulmaz; bu kullanıcılara hiçbir zaman kişiselleştirilmiş reklam gösterilmez ve oyunu iyileştirme verileri toplanmaz. 13 yaşın altındaki bir çocuğun Oyun ile bağlantılı olarak kişisel veri sağladığını düşünüyorsanız lütfen bizimle iletişime geçiniz; bu verilerin silinmesi için gerekli adımlar atılacaktır.
