Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Last updated: 13 October 2026
Applies to the mobile game “Gate Defenders” (package name: com.yemregames.gatedefenders)

Summary. Gate Defenders does not collect, process or share personal data. The Game does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile game Gate Defenders (the “Game”), published by Yemre Games (“we”, “us” or “our”). This Policy applies to the current version of the Game distributed through Google Play.

2. Data Controller and Contact Details

Yemre Games is the developer name used by Yunus Emre Güngör, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who acts as the data controller for the purposes of this Policy. Any enquiries regarding this Policy may be sent to yemregames.dev@gmail.com.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores your gameplay progress (including completed levels, stars, in-game gold, upgrades, story progress and gameplay statistics such as enemies defeated, bosses defeated and total play time) and your settings (including sound effects, ambient sound, vibration, language and the performance display) locally on your device only. This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process or share personal data through the Game. In particular, the Game does not:

  • require registration or the creation of an account;
  • display advertisements;
  • use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services;
  • request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other personal files;
  • collect your name, email address, telephone number or advertising identifier.

5. Google Play

The Game is distributed through Google Play, which is operated by Google. Information relating to your download and use of the Game may be processed by Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. Where you have enabled the sharing of usage and diagnostics data on your device, Google may provide us with aggregated and anonymised statistics concerning the stability and performance of the Game (for example, crash and “application not responding” rates). These statistics do not enable us to identify individual users.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal data in connection with the Game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete such data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your place of residence, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye’s Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), such as the right to obtain information about whether your personal data is processed, the right to request rectification or erasure of your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. As we do not collect or hold personal data relating to you through the Game, we have no such data to disclose, rectify or erase; however, you may contact us at any time with any privacy-related enquiry or request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Should a future version of the Game introduce features that rely on third-party services (for example, advertising or analytics), we will update this Policy before that version is released, describe the categories of data processed and the purposes of such processing, and obtain your consent where required by applicable law. The “Last updated” date at the top of this Policy indicates when it was last revised.

9. Contact

For any questions or requests concerning this Policy or your privacy, please contact us at yemregames.dev@gmail.com.
