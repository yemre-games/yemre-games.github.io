Applies to the mobile game “Dice Bastion” (package name: com.yemregames.dicebastion)

Summary.

  • The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or telephone number. Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Personalized ads are shown only with your explicit consent and never to users under 18. Otherwise ads are non-personalized.
  • With your explicit consent only (and never for users under 18), the Game sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. This is switched off until you agree.
  • You can change your choices at any time under Settings → Ad Preferences.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how personal data is processed in connection with the mobile game Dice Bastion (the “Game”), published by Yemre Games (“we”, “us” or “our”). It applies to the version of the Game distributed through Google Play.

2. Data Controller and Contact Details

Yemre Games is the developer name used by Yunus Emre Güngör, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the processing described in this Policy. You can contact us at yemregames.dev@gmail.com.

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party:

  • your gameplay progress (levels, stars, in-game currencies, upgrades, daily rewards) and settings (for example audio, vibration, language, screen effects and notifications);
  • the year of birth you enter when you first start the Game. It is used only on your device to choose ads that are appropriate for your age and to decide which questions you are asked; it is not sent anywhere;
  • a record of your privacy choices (what you chose, the version of the text you answered and the date), so that we can respect and demonstrate your choices;
  • the last game settings file downloaded from our website (see section 7).
  • If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device; no data is sent for this. You can turn them off in the Game’s settings or in your device settings.

This information is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is financed by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects from your device: your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), information about your interactions with ads (for example ad views, clicks and rewards) and diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK. Google processes this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Personalized ads (ads based on your interests) are shown only if you are 18 or older and have given your explicit consent. The Game asks you this in a separate question with equal Yes and No options; users under 18 are never asked.
  • If you do not consent, or if you are under 18, ads are still shown in the Game, but they are non-personalized: they are selected on the basis of the content of the Game and your approximate location, and your advertising ID is used only for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • For users who indicate an age under 18 we request age-appropriate ad treatment from Google (under 13: treatment as a child; 13–17: treatment as a teen), and all ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (maximum content rating “PG”).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: your choices are collected through Google’s consent form (a Google-certified consent management platform using the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework), which appears instead of our own question. If you do not give consent there, Google may show only limited ads, which do not use your advertising ID for ad selection.
  • Ads are never shown in the middle of a battle. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

5. Game Improvement Data (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics)

If you are 18 or older and give your explicit consent to “Game improvement data”, the Game sends the following information to Google through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. This collection is switched off by default and starts only after you agree; it is never switched on for users under 18.

  • Gameplay events: for example levels started and finished (result, wave reached, duration, stars, continues used), tutorial steps, in-game currency earned and spent, daily rewards claimed, rewarded ads watched, planets unlocked and endless mode results; and general settings of the Game (language, the highest level reached, the number of dice, the version of the game settings file).
  • Identifiers and technical data: app instance ID and Firebase installation ID, device model and operating system version, app version, language, session information, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. The advertising ID is used by Analytics only if you have also consented to personalized ads.
  • Crash reports: when the Game crashes or an error occurs, the error’s stack trace, relevant app state, device metadata (for example model, operating system version, available memory) and a Crashlytics installation ID.

We use this information only to understand how the Game is played (for example, at which level players get stuck), to improve game balance and to find and fix errors. We do not send your name, e-mail address, year of birth or precise location. The data is used in aggregated form; we do not try to identify individual players. Google processes the data as our service provider under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase. In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, the consent choices you make in Google’s consent form also limit how Analytics may store and use identifiers (Google’s consent mode).

6. Data Transmitted Before Your Decision

Until you answer the “Game improvement data” question, no gameplay statistics or crash reports are sent. Gameplay events that occur before you answer (for example, the start of the first level) are kept only temporarily in the device’s memory; they are sent only if you consent and are discarded if you do not or if you close the Game before answering.

7. Game Settings File

When the Game starts, it downloads a small file containing game settings (for example difficulty values and the interval between ads) from our website hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), so that game balance can be adjusted without an update. No identifier is sent with this request; as with any internet request, your IP address and basic technical information (for example the app name and version) are received by the hosting provider, which may process them in accordance with the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Everyone receives the same file.

8. Google Play

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information relating to your download and use of the Game in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. If you have enabled the sharing of usage and diagnostics data on your device, Google may provide us with aggregated statistics on the stability and performance of the Game (for example crash and “application not responding” rates) that do not identify individual users.
