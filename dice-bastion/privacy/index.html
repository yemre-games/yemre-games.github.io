Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Applies to the mobile game “Dice Bastion” (package com.yemregames.dicebastion)

In short: Dice Bastion does not collect personal data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

1. Who is responsible

Dice Bastion is published by Yemre Games (Yunus Emre Güngör, Türkiye). Contact: yemregames.dev@gmail.com.

2. What the game stores

Your game progress (levels, stars, crystals, upgrades, daily rewards) and settings (sound levels, vibration, language) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to third parties, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

3. What the game does not do

The current version of Dice Bastion does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, does not include analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos.

4. Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, which processes information under Google's Privacy Policy. If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data, Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics about the game's stability and performance (for example crash and freeze rates). We cannot identify you from this information.

5. Children

Dice Bastion is made for players aged 16 and over (the target audience declared on Google Play). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Because the game does not collect personal data, we hold no data about you. You can still contact us at yemregames.dev@gmail.com with any privacy question or request, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes

If a future version adds features that use third-party services (for example ads or analytics), we will update this policy before that version is released, describe what is processed and why, and ask for your consent where the law requires it. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest change.
